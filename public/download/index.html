Install on a Mac

  1. Open MM-RYTM-Installer-Mac.pkg and follow the steps: Continue, Agree, Install. macOS asks for your password once.
  2. If macOS says it cannot verify the developer: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway", then open the installer again. Still blocked? In Terminal: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/MM-RYTM-Installer-Mac.pkg
  3. Restart your DAW and rescan the plug-ins. In Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins, turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders", then Option-click Rescan.
  4. Find it under Plug-Ins > Maudite Machine. If your DAW says it is damaged or blocked: in Terminal, sudo xattr -cr /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/MM-RYTM.vst3, then restart the DAW.

macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. The plugin goes to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.

Install on Windows

  1. Run MM-RYTM-Installer-Windows.exe and follow the steps.
  2. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" window shows up: More info > Run anyway.
  3. Restart your DAW and rescan the plug-ins. In Ableton Live: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins, turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders", then Rescan.
  4. Find it under Plug-Ins > Maudite Machine.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The plugin goes to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3; uninstall it from Settings > Apps. Its window uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, part of Windows 11 and up-to-date Windows 10. If something goes wrong, tell me.

The installers are not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, hence the extra click the first time. Any VST3 host: Ableton Live, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase, FL Studio. Logic Pro needs Audio Units, not available yet.

Free to use in your music, commercial releases included. Please share the plugin page, mauditemachine.com/mm-rytm, rather than the installer itself. Both installers in one zip.
