@NAME@, free VST3 plugin by Maudite Machine
https://mauditemachine.com

INSTALL
Mac (macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel):
  Open @NAME@-Installer-Mac.pkg and follow the steps. macOS asks for your password once.
  If macOS says it cannot verify the developer: System Settings > Privacy & Security,
  scroll down, click "Open Anyway", then open the installer again.
[[WIN]]
Windows (10 or 11, 64-bit, beta):
  Run @NAME@-Installer-Windows.exe and follow the steps.
  If a blue "Windows protected your PC" window shows up: More info > Run anyway.
[[/WIN]][[NOWIN]]
Windows: coming soon.
[[/NOWIN]]
The installers are not signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, hence the extra click the first time.

Then restart your DAW and rescan the plug-ins. Ableton Live: turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in
System Folders" in the Plug-Ins settings, then Rescan (Option-click on a Mac).
@NAME@ shows up under Plug-Ins > Maudite Machine.

Any VST3 host works: Ableton Live, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase, FL Studio.
Logic Pro needs Audio Units, not available yet.

UNINSTALL
Mac: delete /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/@NAME@.vst3
[[WIN]]Windows: Settings > Apps > @NAME@ (VST3) > Uninstall
[[/WIN]]
If you installed an older version by hand in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, delete that copy.
