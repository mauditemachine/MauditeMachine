## MM-STUDIO by Maudite Machine: a drum machine, a synth and DJ decks in a free 3D studio in the browser

Montpellier, France, [DATE A FIXER]

Maudite Machine, a DJ and producer based in Montpellier, has turned the home page of his website into MM-STUDIO, a 3D studio you play in the browser. Its three machines share one tempo: the MM-RYTM drum machine, the MM-ARP synth and the MM-DECKS DJ decks. It is free, with no account and nothing to install, and he designed and built all of it.

MM-RYTM has eight voices, 16 steps with parameter locks and 16 patterns. Each voice has a synth layer, a sample layer (with his own kick and snare samples) and its own effects.

MM-ARP has two oscillators and a sub, a ladder filter, overdrive, and an arpeggiator that plays eight chord pads in F sharp minor.

MM-DECKS has two to four decks with a sampler and tempo sync, and a mixer (three band EQ, gain from -12 to +12 dB, VU meters). Tracks load from Maudite Machine's SoundCloud, Creative Commons tracks, your own SoundCloud account or files that stay on your device.

Every parameter can be mapped with MIDI LEARN, and for a Melbourne Instruments Roto-Control the MIDI panel gives ready-made setup files.

MM-STUDIO needs WebGL 2, and MIDI works in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Firefox. On a phone it shows one machine at a time. Every control has a labelled HTML twin for keyboard and screen reader users.

Indie dance and dark disco with a psychedelic edge. DJ, producer, founder of VRSTL Records. Fifteen years in the Montréal underground, now based in the south of France.
